CostThe Cloudflare cost ladder

Start on the free allowances. One paid step, and it changes nothing else.

Mallok itself is free and open source, and it hosts nothing: what you pay, you pay to Cloudflare (and to Resend if you send email) from your own accounts. Mallok is designed to start on Cloudflare's free allowances. That is a design target — not a promise of unlimited free service.

01The two rungs

Free at first; $5 a month per account when there is volume.

Every Cloudflare product Mallok depends on has a free tier. The one paid step is Workers Paid. It raises limits on the same Worker, the same D1 database and the same R2 bucket — no migration, no architecture change.

STEP 0 $0 to Cloudflare

Free allowances

100k Worker requests a day at 10 ms CPU each, 5 GB of D1, 10 GB of R2, free caching and purging, five cron triggers per account, Turnstile and DNS. You still need a domain, which is not free.

STEP 1 $5 / month / account

Workers Paid

10M requests and 30M CPU-milliseconds a month, 30 s of CPU per invocation by default, far higher D1 limits and 250 cron triggers. Charged per Cloudflare account, so it covers every site in that account. Usage beyond the included amounts is billed by Cloudflare.

03Limits that shape the product

The free tier has edges. Here is where they are.

Five cron triggers per account

Each Mallok site uses one cron trigger for scheduled publishing and email retries. One free Cloudflare account therefore carries scheduled work for at most five sites; a larger portfolio needs Workers Paid.

D1's daily cap is a hard stop

Exceeding D1's free daily rows makes the database unavailable for the rest of that day, and with it everything that needs D1: cold renders, saves and inquiries. Watch usage as a site grows.

10 ms of CPU on the free plan

Cache hits measured 0–1 ms of CPU on rc.5. Cold renders measured p50 11 ms and p95 35 ms, above the 10 ms target in 16 of 30 samples; none failed in that sample. This is the release candidates' known limitation, and those rc.5 figures do not certify later candidates. Release status

A custom domain is required for caching

Edge caching — the thing that keeps nearly every request off the Worker's CPU budget — works on a custom domain. A workers.dev address is for preview only. Budget for a domain; Cloudflare Registrar sells them at cost.

04Deliberately unused

What Mallok leaves out to keep the ladder short.

A dependency with a tight free tier or no free tier would add a second paid step. So the Worker uses D1, R2, Static Assets, the Cache API and one cron trigger — and nothing else.

05What is not free

The honest total.

Your domain

Needed for caching and for a credible company site. Priced by your registrar.

Email at volume

Inquiry email goes through Resend. Its free tier covers 3,000 emails a month and 100 a day; beyond that is Resend's paid plan.

Your time

Setup needs someone comfortable with Node and a terminal. Themes, plugins and upgrades need a redeploy by that same person.

One command. Your account. Your content.

Mallok creates a Worker, a D1 database and an R2 bucket in your own Cloudflare account and deploys the site there. Nothing is hosted by Mallok, and there is no Mallok account to open.

npx mallok create my-site

Needs Node.js 22+ and wrangler login. Add --no-deploy to generate and check a project locally without creating cloud resources. Currently a 0.1 release candidate.
