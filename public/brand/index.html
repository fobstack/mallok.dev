BrandBrand kit v0.2

The Kinetic Thread M.

Mallok's logo, favicon set, colours and type, with the rules for using them. Every file on this page is a copy from the brand kit and can be downloaded as it is.

01Concept

One unbroken thread, and one dot that travels.

The initial M is drawn as a single continuous thread, standing for the seamless serverless flow of a site that runs on Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2. The only teal element, the shuttle dot at the lower right, answers the two flowing l's in the wordmark.

Together they tell the product's story: no operations, content woven live the moment it is saved, and Markdown that is free to leave.

02Logo files

Logo, mark and wordmark.

All SVGs are pure vector paths with no font dependency. Use the colour logo wherever you can: the light version on light pages, the dark version on dark ones. The mono versions are for print, white papers and partner walls.

04Usage

How to use it, and the red lines.

How to use

  • Clear space: keep at least one shuttle-dot diameter free on every side of the logo.
  • Minimum size: the full logo at least 96 px wide on screen; the mark at least 16 × 16 px.
  • From 16 to 32 px, use the versions in the favicon set, which are drawn for small sizes.
  • Pick the file for the background: colour-light or mono-light on light; colour-dark, accent-dark or mono-dark on dark.
  • Teal is the one story accent: the shuttle dot, the double l, and “instant” status. Use it sparingly.

Things to avoid

  • Do not put the M on a rounded tile or any other container. The only exception is the iOS and Android app icons.
  • Do not stretch or distort the logo.
  • Do not change the balance of teal and purple, or recolour either.
  • Do not use Electric Teal (#06B6D4) or #0D9488 for small text on light backgrounds; they are below 4.5:1 there. This site uses --live (#0f766e) for teal text.

07UI language

How the logo becomes an interface.

The ribbon becomes the threads that connect cards and diagram nodes. Its rounded ends become pill buttons and rounded cards. The shuttle dot marks status, list items and the current page. The wordmark's twin l becomes paired things: the two timelines, EN and 中文.

80% neutral · Cloud White / Midnight15% purple

80% neutral, 15% Kinetic Purple for actions and threads, 5% Electric Teal for one story only: live, instant, arrived. Teal is never a second button colour.

08Type

Figtree for Latin text, system fonts for Chinese and code.

Figtree's 800 weight is the closest match to the wordmark's heavy geometric lowercase, and its 400 reads well as body text. The font files are not in /assets/fonts/ yet, so this build shows the system fallback; no request leaves the site either way. Chinese uses PingFang SC, Microsoft YaHei and Noto Sans SC; code uses each platform's monospace. Neither loads a web font.

One command. Your account. Your content.

Mallok creates a Worker, a D1 database and an R2 bucket in your own Cloudflare account and deploys the site there. Nothing is hosted by Mallok, and there is no Mallok account to open.

npx mallok create my-site

Needs Node.js 22+ and wrangler login. Add --no-deploy to generate and check a project locally without creating cloud resources. Currently a 0.1 release candidate.
